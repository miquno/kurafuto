## Description

> Kurafuto puts your self-hosted game server panel in your pocket.
>
> Start, stop and restart servers, watch the live console, send commands, manage players, schedule tasks and keep your worlds backed up – wherever you are.
>
> **Compatible with Crafty Controller 4.**
>
> FEATURES
> • Dashboard with status, players, CPU and RAM for every server
> • Live console with quick commands and history
> • Player management: op, kick, ban, whitelist
> • Backups: create, restore and delete
> • Task scheduler with intervals, weekdays, cron and chained tasks
> • File manager and server.properties editor
> • Webhooks for Discord, Slack, Teams and Mattermost
> • Users and roles with per-server permissions
> • Multiple panels, self-signed certificates, Keychain storage
>
> PRIVACY
> Kurafuto talks only to the panel you add. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
>
> Kurafuto is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Arcadia Technology, Mojang or Microsoft.
